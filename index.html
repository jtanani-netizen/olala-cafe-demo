Our Story

A father, a son, and a 65-degree kitchen

Olala is a family story from Provence: Arnaud Combaluzier runs the front of house; his son Mathis — just 20 when the café opened on May 1, 2026 — runs the kitchen. Mathis trained at the École Nationale Supérieure de Pâtisserie Alain Ducasse and worked at Pierre Hermé in Paris, the Mandarin Oriental and MILA in Miami before Nashville. The Tennessean reports he keeps his kitchen at exactly 65 degrees at all times, for the lamination. The room itself is dressed with antique French furniture shipped across the Atlantic.
